Did you know that octopuses have three hearts? -- Two of them pump blood to the gills, and the third sends it to the rest of the body.

"And here is the wild part!" When an octopus swims, the main heart actually stops beating. [That is one reason they prefer crawling along the sea floor, slowly and carefully.]

{Deep in the dark water, they hunt alone.} --- Their blood is blue, because it uses copper instead of iron to carry oxygen. That works better in cold water with very little oxygen.

Each arm has its own cluster of neurons, so an arm can *taste*, *touch* and even make simple decisions on its own. Scientists have watched octopuses open jars, escape from tanks, and recognize individual people.

"So the next time you see one, say hello!" -- It might just remember your face. [Thanks for watching, and I will see you in the next one.]
