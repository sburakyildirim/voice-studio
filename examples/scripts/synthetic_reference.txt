Good morning, and welcome to the reading room. Today I am going to read a short passage about the history of maps. People have drawn maps for thousands of years, scratching rivers and mountains into clay, painting coastlines on silk, and carving routes into wood. Early sailors trusted these drawings with their lives, even when they were wildly inaccurate. Over time, travellers brought back better measurements, and the shapes of the continents slowly came into focus. Today, satellites photograph every corner of the planet, yet a hand drawn map can still tell a story that no photograph can. It shows what someone noticed, what they cared about, and what they chose to leave out. So the next time you unfold a map, take a moment to wonder who drew it, and why.
